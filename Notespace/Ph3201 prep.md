### **Chapter Weighting & Triage Analysis**
* **Quantum Statistics (Bosons, Fermions, Phonons): ~42% Weight.** Highly tested. You are guaranteed questions on BEC, Fermi gases, and the Debye model.
* **Ensemble Theory (Canonical & Grand/Gibbs): ~40% Weight.** Extremely consistent. Expect questions calculating partition functions, average energies, and heat capacities for non-standard systems.
* **Probability Theory: ~18% Weight.** Easy, guaranteed points. There is almost always a question on changing variables or finding a cumulative distribution function (CDF).
* **Low Yield / Skip for Now:** Thermodynamics review, Master Equations, H-Theorem, and detailed Microcanonical derivations.

### **The 40-Hour Scientific Study Plan**
To maximize retention while exhausted, use **50/10 time blocks**: 50 minutes of hyper-focused reading/problem solving, followed by a strict 10-minute break. Keep your meals quick and manageable in your room so you don't lose momentum.
