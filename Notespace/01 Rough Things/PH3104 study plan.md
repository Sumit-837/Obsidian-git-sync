# Study plan

## 🔑 **Most Important / High-Yield Topics**

These are the things examiners **love to ask** because they combine concepts, derivations, and numerical problems:

### Lec 0–2 (Basics of AC & Waveforms)

- **LCR circuit resonance** (condition, impedance minimum, phase = 0, Q-factor) → **guaranteed**.
    
- **AC waveform**: RMS, average, instantaneous power.
    
- **Why AC is sinusoidal** (Faraday’s law derivation).
    
- **RMS value derivation** and application.
    
- **Rectifiers (half-wave, full-wave)** – working, waveforms, efficiency, ripple factor.
    
- **Single/Three-phase systems** – equations, power factor, star vs delta connection (they love problems here).
    

### Lec 3 (R-C Circuits & Filters)

- **Phasor diagram of RC circuit**.
    
- **Impedance and phase relation**.
    
- **Low-pass and high-pass filters (RC lag/lead networks)**.
    
- **Variation of Z and phase with frequency**.
    

### Lec 4–5 (R-L, RLC, Quality Factor, Kirchhoff’s laws)

- **Charging/discharging of capacitor (RC time constant)**.
    
- **R-L circuit transient response**.
    
- **LCR circuit at resonance + Quality factor (Q = ω₀L/R)**.
    
- **Kirchhoff’s laws** and **circuit approximation** → always basics for numericals.
    

### Lec 6–8 (Circuit Theorems & Analysis)

- **Node analysis method** (exam questions often test you here).
    
- **Superposition theorem**.
    
- **Thevenin & Norton equivalents** (standard numerical problems).
    

### Lec 9 (Fourier Series & Transform)

- **Fourier series** for square/triangular/sawtooth waves (at least formulas).
    
- **Fourier Transform basics + inverse** (simple conceptual questions).
    

### Lec 10–11 (Semiconductors)

- **Semiconductors basics** (intrinsic carrier concentration, energy bands).
    
- **Quantum tunneling** (concept + application to semiconductors/diodes).
    
- **Density of states & dispersion relation** (band structure).
    

---

## 📉 **Moderately Important**

- **Two-phase system** (rarely asked in detail, sometimes skipped).
    
- **Some deeper Fourier transform properties** (usually just basics expected).
    
- **Intuitive circuit rules** (good for understanding, less likely as direct Q).

## 🎯 **Exam Trend (What usually comes)**

- **Numerical Problems**:
    
    - RMS values, rectifiers (efficiency/ripple factor).
        
    - Resonance in RLC (calculate Q, bandwidth, power factor).
        
    - Thevenin/Norton equivalents.
        
    - Node/mesh analysis.
        
- **Derivations**:
    
    - Resonance condition.
        
    - RMS derivation.
        
    - Charging/discharging capacitor.
        
    - Fourier series expansion for square wave.
        
- **Conceptual/Short Questions**:
    
    - Why AC is sinusoidal.
        
    - Difference between star & delta connection.
        
    - Applications of filters.
        
    - What is density of states, tunneling, band structure.
