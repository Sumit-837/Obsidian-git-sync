# Quantum Mechanics: Scattering Theory Roadmap

## Phase 1: Rebuilding the Foundation (1D Basics)
- [ ] **The Free Particle**
  - **Source:** [[Introduction to Quantum Mec_3rdEd_ (Z-Library).pdf#page=74|Griffith's]] (Sec 2.4)
  - **Goal:** Understand pla ne waves ($e^{ikx}$) as the starting point for free scattering states.
- [ ] **The Delta-Function Potential**
  - **Source:** [[Introduction to Quantum Mec_3rdEd_ (Z-Library).pdf|Griffith's]] (Sec 2.5) & [[Book-PH3102-QM-S.Lal.pdf|Lal]]
  - **Goal:** Master boundary conditions. Understand why the wavefunction is continuous but its derivative has a discontinuity.

## Phase 2: Intro to 1D Scattering
- [ ] **Reflection & Transmission**
  - **Source:** [[(3)parity symmetric basis for scattering (st 6.1.2).pdf|3]] (Sec 6.1 - 6.1.1)
  - **Goal:** Calculate reflection ($r$) and transmission ($t$) amplitudes for simple barriers.
- [ ] **Parity Basis & S-Matrix**
  - **Source:** [[(3)parity symmetric basis for scattering (st 6.1.2).pdf|3]] (Sec 6.1.2 - 6.1.3)
  - **Goal:** Use even and odd parity to simplify math for symmetric potentials. Learn how to construct the 2x2 S-matrix.

## Phase 3: The Complex S-Matrix (Advanced 1D)
- [ ] **Analyticity & Poles**
  - **Source:** [[(2a)Analyticity properties of S materix.pdf|2a]]
  - **Goal:** Push momentum into the complex plane ($k = i\kappa$). Prove that an S-matrix pole mathematically equals a physical bound state.
- [ ] **Resonances & Lifetimes**
  - **Source:** [[(2b)resooances and life time.pdf|2b]]
  - **Goal:** Understand quasi-bound states. Connect zeros in the transmission denominator to the Breit-Wigner resonance shape and particle lifetimes.
- [ ] **Fano Resonances & Interference**
  - **Source:** [[(2d)T-Stub-Anti-resonance.pdf|2d]]
  - **Goal:** Analyze asymmetric resonance profiles caused by continuum-discrete interference. Plot $T(kL)$ to visualize the anti-resonance.

## Phase 4: Periodic Structures
- [ ] **Kronig-Penney Model**
  - **Source:** [[(2c)Kronig–Penney-S Matrix.pdf|2c]]
  - **Goal:** Apply the Transfer Matrix method to an infinite lattice of delta barriers. Observe how discrete resonances merge into continuous energy bands.

## Phase 5: 3D Scattering (The Main Event)
- [ ] **3D Math Prerequisites**
  - **Source:** [[Introduction to Quantum Mec_3rdEd_ (Z-Library).pdf|Griffith's]] (Sec 4.1.1 & 4.1.2)
  - **Goal:** Quickly review the angular equation and Spherical Harmonics ($Y_l^m$).
- [ ] **Cross Sections & Geometry**
  - **Source:** [[Introduction to Quantum Mec_3rdEd_ (Z-Library).pdf|Griffith's]] (Sec 10.1 & 10.2)
  - **Goal:** Define impact parameter, solid angle ($d\Omega$), and the scattering amplitude $f(\theta)$.
- [ ] **Partial Wave Expansion**
  - **Source:** [[(3)parity symmetric basis for scattering (st 6.1.2).pdf|3]] (Sec 6.2.3 - 6.2.6)
  - **Goal:** Mathematically slice an incoming plane wave into expanding spherical waves. Calculate the phase shift ($\delta_l$) for each channel.
- [ ] **The Born Approximation**
  - **Source:** [[Introduction to Quantum Mec_3rdEd_ (Z-Library).pdf|Griffith's]] (Sec 10.4) & [[(3)parity symmetric basis for scattering (st 6.1.2).pdf|3]] (Sec 6.3)
  - **Goal:** Learn the perturbative method for weak potentials. Prove that the scattering amplitude is the 3D Fourier transform of the potential.
